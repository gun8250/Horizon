# US consumer profile

Use this profile when an item's primary value is understanding US consumer demand, a consumer company's operating trajectory, or the macro forces (rates, tariffs, income) that drive US consumer spending.

Watched subjects and their signals:

- **Athleisure & apparel brands**: Lululemon (comps, guidance, markdowns, product launches, CEO/management changes, China expansion), Alo Yoga, Vuori, Nike, Gap, and direct competitors; market-share and price-positioning shifts among them.
- **US consumption macro**: retail sales (headline and control group), consumer confidence and sentiment surveys, personal spending and saving rate, credit card delinquencies, student-loan and household balance-sheet developments.
- **Policy drivers**: Fed rate decisions and guidance when framed around the consumer or discretionary spending, tariff changes on apparel/footwear imports, China-US trade developments affecting retail supply chains.
- **Market context**: systemic US equity events (breadth collapses, liquidity events) that reset consumer-sector valuations.

Also route major retailer earnings with explicit comp/guidance figures (Target, Walmart, Costco, Amazon) as demand read-throughs.

Do not route: routine single-stock price moves without news, crypto, individual analyst ratings without new data, celebrity or lifestyle coverage of brands, non-US consumer markets, or generic market recaps.
