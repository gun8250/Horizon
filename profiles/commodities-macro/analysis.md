# Evaluation goal

Evaluate whether an item gives timely, credible, decision-relevant information about the supply-demand balance, price level, or policy regime of a watched commodity chain (oil shipping, phosphate/fertilizer, uranium, tungsten, aluminum, coal) or its direct macro drivers.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Regime change.** Policy regime shifts (export bans/quota releases, sanctions imposing or lifting, capacity policy changes), record or threshold-crossing price levels with a stated baseline (e.g. long-term contract at historic high), production guidance cuts/raises of >5% from a major producer, hard supply disruption (mine/plant closure, force majeure).
- **7-8: Material data.** Concrete monthly/quarterly data with figures and baselines (export volumes, imports, production, scrapping, orders), OPEC+ decisions, subsidy or tender outcomes, new long-term contract price points, confirmed new capacity with dates and tonnage.
- **5-6: Useful context.** Credible analyst estimates of supply/demand with explicit numbers and method, price moves with a stated driver and baseline, minor producer news.
- **3-4: Low value.** Routine daily/weekly price recaps without new drivers, vague forecasts without numbers ("prices expected to rise"), single-bank price target notes, conference commentary.
- **0-2: Noise.** Rumors, promotional or sponsored content, unexplained numbers, sensational predictions, items about unrelated companies that merely share a keyword.

# Evaluation guidance

Reward specific numbers with units and baselines ($/day TCE, $/lb U3O8, $/tonne, million tonnes, vessels, % yoy). A price move deserves a higher score only when the item states the level, the change, and the driver. Distinguish reported data from forecasts. Reported policy must come from an official or clearly attributed source. Do not reward breadth of coverage alone — a narrow but concrete data point about a watched chain beats a broad generic commodities roundup.

Use three to five specific topic tags, e.g. `tanker-rates`, `vlcc`, `uranium-longterm`, `china-export-quota`, `dap-india`, `tungsten-apt`.
