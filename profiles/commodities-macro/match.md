# Commodities & macro profile

Use this profile when an item's primary value is a supply-demand or price development in one of the watched commodity chains, or a policy/macro event that directly drives them.

Watched chains and their signals:

- **Oil shipping / tankers**: VLCC, Suezmax, Aframax, tanker rates, TCE (time charter equivalent), OPEC+ production and quota decisions, oil sanctions and shadow-fleet actions, shipbuilding orders, scrapping/demolition volumes, newbuild prices.
- **Phosphate & fertilizer**: DAP/MAP prices and exports, phosphate rock, Indian fertilizer subsidy and DAP import tenders, sulfur and sulfuric acid prices, Chinese export inspection/quota policy, monoammonium phosphate.
- **Uranium & nuclear fuel**: uranium spot and long-term contract prices, Kazatomprom, Cameco, uranium production guidance, enrichment (SWU), nuclear reactor restarts and new-build approvals, AI datacenter power contracts with nuclear plants.
- **Tungsten**: tungsten concentrate and APT (ammonium paratungstate) prices, Chinese mining quotas and export controls, overseas strategic stockpiling, tungsten carbide and cutting-tool demand.
- **Aluminum**: Chinese electrolytic aluminum capacity ceiling and capacity-swap policy, operating capacity and utilization rates, alumina prices, Indonesian and Middle-East smelter projects.
- **Coal**: thermal and coking coal prices, Chinese import volumes, production safety crackdowns, Indian and SEA demand.

Also route cross-cutting drivers: El Nino / La Nina and monsoon outlooks (fertilizer and energy demand), USD and Fed policy shifts when framed as commodity drivers, broad trade/export-control regimes covering these goods.

Do not route: routine equity market moves, earnings of companies outside these chains, generic macro commentary without a commodity linkage, tech news, crypto, or promotional trading content.
